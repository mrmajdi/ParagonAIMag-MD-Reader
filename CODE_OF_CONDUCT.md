# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Paragon AI MAG MD Reader to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity, experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Welcome questions, including beginner questions and questions in Persian, Arabic or any other language.
- Respect privacy: do not ask others to share private documents, tokens or personal data.
- Accept constructive feedback and apologise when you cause harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory jokes, sexualized attention, doxxing, publishing private information, threats, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or ban contributors who violate this policy. Report concerns privately to the maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
